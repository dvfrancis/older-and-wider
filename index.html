Dear OWLs

We are two women of a certain age who talk far too much, mostly about our bodies, our families and whatever went wrong this week. Nothing is off limits, and very little of it is dignified.

Pop the kettle on and join us. You are an OWL now, an Older and Wider Listener, whether you like it or not.

Love, Jenny and Judith
