# Home page

Scope: index.html, the home page. Mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: existing listeners (OWLs) and new listeners. First job: play the latest episode. Second job: book the 2027 tour.
Constraints: static HTML and CSS, no inline styles or scripts, no mailing-list form, no carousel.
Chosen direction: "The Weekly Magazine" (chosen by the owner from five built options, 27 Nov 2026), with the bingo-ticket episode picker borrowed from the Bingo option.
Memorable moment: the cover. The lead coverline is the Play button.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is this week's issue of a 1980s British women's weekly, and the hosts are the cover stars. It refuses the category default of a podcast hero with a player and a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Pink #F492BF cover ground. Masthead in Anton capitals: OLDER and WIDER in red #A60201, AND in magenta #E8046C (logotype). Coverlines as solid slabs in red, magenta, white and blue #6771DD, set in Barlow Condensed capitals. Yellow flash roundel. Photos carry a fine halftone dot screen. Back issues sit on a bingo ticket grid with magenta dabs.

STORY: The visitor sees the two hosts on this week's cover, reads the lead coverline as the newest episode, and presses it to play. The roundel tells them the 2027 tour is on sale. Below, they pick a back issue on the bingo ticket, then book a show.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim red nav strip. Masthead across the full width. Issue line under it. Cover photo of both hosts with halftone screen. Lead coverline slab with play icon, inside the first 844px on a phone. Yellow LIVE 2027 roundel on the photo corner.
