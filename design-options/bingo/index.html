Who's calling the numbers

We're Jenny Eclair and Judith Holder. Every week we sit down and moan, laugh and overshare about getting older, because somebody has to.

There is a round of hypochondriac's bingo, and we answer your questions on air. If you listen, you're an OWL, which is short for Older & Wider Listener, and we're very glad you came.
