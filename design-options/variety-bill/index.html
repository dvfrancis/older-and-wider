About the show

We are two women of a certain age, talking every week about what getting older does to us. Nothing is off limits and very little of it is dignified, which is most of the fun.

Every episode has the news from both our households and a round of hypochondriac's bingo. Then we open the post for the listener Q&A. Our listeners are the OWLs, and they send far better questions than we deserve. Some of them we can even answer.

The live show is the podcast in a theatre, with you in it. Come along and take part with other OWLs, join in the games and put your question to us in person, where we can't pretend the line has gone.

If you want to take a bit of us home, the shop sells mugs, tea towels and badges at olderandwidershop.com.

Nominated for the British Podcast Awards, Listeners' Choice 2022.
